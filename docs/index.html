Orbit Lock

One button. Every planet.

Orbit Lock is a one-button gravity puzzle. Your orb is pulled toward the active planet. Tap to switch the pull to the next planet, swing through the field, and reach the gold ring before your taps run out. 500 levels, every one proven solvable, with new twists along the way: giants and moons, repulsors, wormholes, blinking walls, numbered rings, bouncy walls and wrap-around edges.

How to play

Tap to launch. Tap again to switch which planet pulls you (the dashed ring marks who pulls next). Planets, walls and the edge of space are all fatal. Pass through every white ring before the goal. Each level gives you exactly the taps it needs.
