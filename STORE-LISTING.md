## Description
```
Orbit Lock is a one-button gravity puzzle.

Your orb is pulled toward one planet at a time. Tap to switch the pull to the
next planet. That is the whole control scheme, but the orb keeps its momentum,
so every tap is a decision: too early and you drift into space, too late and
you crash.

Reach the gold ring before your taps run out. Every level gives you exactly
as many taps as it needs, and not one more.

• 500 levels, every one proven solvable
• One-button, pick-up-and-play controls
• A ghost trail of your last attempt, so retries are tuning, not guessing
• New twists arrive quietly: walls, checkpoint rings, drifting planets, heavy
  giants and dead moons, repulsors, wormholes, blinking pulsar walls, numbered
  rings, a drifting goal, bouncy walls and wrap-around edges
• A winding star map that remembers how far you've come
• No ads, no accounts, no data collection. Works offline.

From the maker of Tidal Orbit.
```
