How to Play

  • Tap to launch the orb.
  • Tap again to switch the pull to the next planet (the one with the dashed ring).
  • Reach the gold ring.
  • Planets, walls and the edge of space are all fatal.
  • Fewer taps = more stars. Your last attempt is drawn as a ghost trail.
